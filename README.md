## Featured Projects

### LawEase: AI Legal Research Assistant (Final Year Project)

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,flask,react,postgres,supabase" alt="Python, Flask, React, PostgreSQL, Supabase" />

- Production RAG pipeline with FAISS vector search over 12,000+ legal documents, delivering roughly 5 second query responses and a 20% gain in answer relevance over baseline GPT prompting in internal testing
- Decoupled Flask REST API and React frontend with PostgreSQL/Supabase schemas and JWT-authenticated multi-role access for clients and advocates
- Improved retrieval precision across a heterogeneous legal corpus through chunking strategies, embedding optimization, and reranking

Repository: [LawEase](https://github.com/AbdulRehman19-bit/REPO-NAME)

### DocLens: Multimodal Document Intelligence

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,fastapi,react,supabase" alt="Python, FastAPI, React, Supabase" />

- Visual RAG system using ColPali embeddings to answer questions grounded directly in tables, charts, and scanned pages, without relying on OCR text alone
- Fuses visual embeddings with BM25 via Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then applies cross-encoder reranking to surface the most relevant page regions
- Full-stack deployment (FastAPI, React, Supabase auth) through a Git-based CI/CD pipeline to Hugging Face Spaces and Netlify

Repository: [DocLens](https://github.com/AbdulRehman19-bit/REPO-NAME)

### PakFinance RAG

<img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,fastapi" alt="Python, FastAPI" />

- Domain-specific question answering over Pakistan's financial and regulatory corpus covering tax law, banking, forex, and capital markets
- Consolidates hundreds of PDFs from PSX, SBP, and FBR into one searchable interface, with every answer grounded in a cited source document and page number
- Answer-quality monitoring with RAGAS evaluation on top of hybrid BM25 and dense retrieval

Repository: [PakFinance RAG](https://github.com/AbdulRehman19-bit/REPO-NAME)
